## Experiment C: field recovery and risk-conditioned detection

**20 independent replications, base seed 7.**

### Field recovery

| Model, fitted on extraction-quality data | PAI mean | 95% interval | SD |
|---|---|---|---|
| District-aggregate baseline (uniform) | 0.46 | [0.10, 0.80] | 0.22 |
| Kernel density on extracted records | 2.95 | [1.86, 3.60] | 0.53 |
| Kernel density plus triggering (ETAS-style) | 2.86 | [1.75, 3.41] | 0.51 |

Mean reporting fraction: 0.483. Mean correlation between recovered and ground-truth field: 0.807 [0.764, 0.852].

**Finding that holds:** the recovered field outperforms district-aggregate resolution
by roughly a factor of six on the mean, with non-overlapping intervals.

**Claim withdrawn:** the previous manuscript reported that adding a triggering
component improved recovery (2.91 against 2.79). Across replications the two are
indistinguishable and the ordering reverses on the mean. Reported as equivalent.

### Risk-conditioned detection

| Sensor condition | Reduction @ 80% recall | 95% interval | Reduction @ 90% recall | 95% interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ambiguous (d′ = 1.2) | 16.2% | [13.0, 19.6] | 12.3% | [9.6, 14.6] |
| Strong (d′ = 2.0) | 15.0% | [10.5, 19.5] | 12.2% | [9.8, 16.1] |

| Sensor condition | AUC fixed | AUC risk-conditioned |
|---|---|---|
| Ambiguous (d′ = 1.2) | 0.802 | 0.831 |
| Strong (d′ = 2.0) | 0.921 | 0.930 |

**Finding that holds:** risk conditioning improves the operating point in both sensor
regimes, by roughly 15 to 16 percent at 80 percent recall.

**Claim withdrawn:** the previous manuscript reported 19.1 percent in the ambiguous
condition against 14.0 percent in the strong condition, and argued that the prior
matters most where sensor evidence is weakest. Across replications the two conditions
differ by about one percentage point with intervals overlapping over almost their
entire range. The previously reported gap was an artifact of a single realization.
